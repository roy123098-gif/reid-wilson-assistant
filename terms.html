Reid & Wilson is an independent educational service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Internal Revenue Service.

Educational use

The EIC assistant, ITIN guide, and budget worksheet provide general educational information. They do not prepare, sign, authenticate, transmit, mail, or file a tax return or Form W-7 and do not provide legal, immigration, tax, investment, or individualized financial advice.

ITIN limits

The ITIN guide builds a preparation checklist only. An ITIN is for federal tax purposes, does not authorize work or change immigration status, and does not qualify a taxpayer for EIC. Verify current Form W-7 instructions and supporting-document rules before applying.

Verify before acting

Results depend on the information entered and may be incomplete. Verify tax results with current IRS forms, instructions, the IRS EITC Assistant, or a qualified tax professional before filing or making a financial decision.

Supported year

The current EIC calculation supports tax year 2025. Rules and dollar limits change by year.

No sensitive information

Do not enter ITINs, Social Security numbers, passport or visa numbers, birth dates, addresses, bank information, passwords, or tax and identity documents. Test Mode should be used with fictional information while evaluating the prototype.

The service may change, become unavailable, or contain errors. These working terms should receive professional legal review before a public commercial launch or paid ITIN service.

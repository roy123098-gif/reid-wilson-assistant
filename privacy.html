Privacy Notice

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Do not enter ITINs, Social Security numbers, passport or visa numbers, birth dates, home addresses, bank information, passwords, or copies of tax and identity documents into this educational prototype.

Information used

The assistant temporarily processes the general income, filing-status, household, budget, goal-planning, and ITIN-checklist choices you enter so it can return an educational result. The ITIN guide uses general selections only and does not need identity-document numbers.

Storage and encryption

In public mode, tax-profile and ITIN-guide details remain only in page memory until the page closes or refreshes and are not intentionally saved as profiles on the server or in browser storage. Savings goals and check-in dates are an exception: when you create a goal vault, they are encrypted in this browser with a key derived from your PIN. The PIN and key are not stored or sent to Reid & Wilson, so a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered. Reset Vault permanently deletes that browser's encrypted goal record. The local desktop tax profile is encrypted at rest with authenticated encryption. Public-mode analytics do not store question text. Hosting and network providers may keep ordinary technical logs such as IP addresses and request times.

Sharing

Reid & Wilson does not sell tax-profile or goal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host and protect the site, or disclosed when legally required.

Your choices

Close or refresh the page to clear public-mode tax-profile data. Use Reset Vault to delete saved goals on that browser. Do not use the tool if you do not want the entered details processed. Contact Reid & Wilson through the website contact page with privacy questions.

Scope

This notice is a working notice for the prototype and should be reviewed by a qualified privacy professional before collecting real taxpayer or identity documents, accepting payments for ITIN help, or launching user accounts.
